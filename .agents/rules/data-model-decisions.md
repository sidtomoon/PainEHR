# Data-model and scope decisions — treat as settled

- **Patient and Encounter are separate, normalized entities.** Never collapse a patient's whole history into one row or flatten encounters into a wide table. A scheduled future visit is *also* a distinct entity (`appointments` table) from a completed encounter, for the same reason.
- **Every AI-extracted field carries a confidence tag** (`high`/`medium`/`low`) and must be clinician-verified before it's treated as the real record. AI output is never silently accepted as ground truth — this same principle extends to the CDSS history/exam prompts (`/api/cdss/prompts`): those are checklist suggestions only, never auto-filled into a field, and the system prompts explicitly forbid suggesting a diagnosis or treatment at that stage.
- **Genuinely categorical fields are Postgres enums** (pain location, pain mechanism, procedure category/guidance/intent, functional change, patient global impression); **narrative fields stay free text** (red flags, functional impact, learning point). This distinction is deliberate — it's the direct fix for the predecessor system's failure mode (see `predecessor-lessons.md`) of using free text where controlled vocabulary was needed for cohort queries, without over-modeling fields that are genuinely narrative.
- **Follow-up/procedure visits carry forward chief complaint, diagnosis, and pain location** from the most recent encounter that actually recorded them (not just the chronologically-last row) — prefilled and editable, not re-typed from scratch. See `src/app/patients/[id]/encounters/new/page.tsx`.
- **Outcome fields are deliberately single-item proxies** (0–10 scores for function/mood/sleep/QoL, a widespread-pain screen, patient global impression, a general adverse-event field) — not full multi-item validated instruments (RMDQ/PROMIS/ODI). This is a considered tradeoff for a busy solo OPD, documented with citations in `V2_SCOPE.md`. Don't "upgrade" this to full questionnaires without discussion.
- **Scope stays narrow by default.** A much larger master spec exists (CDSS stages 4–5, RBAC, teaching mode, multi-institution, research engine) — see `V3_CLINIC_OPERATIONS_AND_CDSS.md` for the full map. Don't scope-creep into it uninvited; the two scope docs at the repo root record what's proposed vs. actually built.
- **CDSS is risk-staged.** Stages 1–2 (history/exam prompts) are low-risk checklist assistance and are built. Stages 4–5 (differential diagnosis, treatment suggestions) are real diagnostic/prescribing recommendations in an opioid-prescribing, procedure-doing specialty — they need citation-backed reasoning and the same "AI-suggested, unverified" marking before being trusted, and shouldn't be built with the same casualness as 1–2.
